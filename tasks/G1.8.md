# G1.8 — State the ARACHNE_* bridge deprecation horizon

## Spec

With every default flipped, the `ARACHNE_*` names survive only through the
two-way bridge in `lib/config.sh`. Write the policy down so consumers can plan:

- `docs/CONFIG.md` gains a "Legacy names" section: the bridge is generic and
  bidirectional, `ARACHNE_X` in the environment still outranks a conf-file
  `TASKPUMP_X`, the bridge is guaranteed through every 0.x release, and it is
  removed at 1.0.0 (a MAJOR change under the contract's own versioning rules).
- The `CHANGELOG.md` 0.1.0 entry states the same horizon in one line.
- The header comment in `lib/config.sh` points at the CONFIG.md section instead
  of re-explaining the transition.

## Scope

Documentation only. No code, no tests beyond docs-consistency checks already in
CI. Do not *remove* any bridging — Arachne consumes it through all of F90.

## Acceptance

- CONFIG.md states the horizon; CHANGELOG states it; config.sh's header points
  at CONFIG.md.
- The three statements agree on the version numbers.
